OpenPunch

Prediction of the punching shear capacity of reinforced concrete flat slabs with and without openings. The Eurocode 2 resistance, with the control perimeter reduced for the openings, is multiplied by a correction factor learned by a CatBoost model from 742 punching tests (185 slabs with openings). The calculation runs in your browser.

Research and preliminary-assessment tool. Predictions combine Eurocode 2 with a correction learned from tests, are reliable only within the range of the experimental database, and do not replace code-based design verification.
Model and inputs
  • Vu = VR,c,EC2 × exp(f), where VR,c,EC2 is the Eurocode 2 (EN 1992-1-1:2004) punching resistance with mean strengths and no partial factors, and f is the correction predicted by a CatBoost model.
  • Openings reduce the basic control perimeter u1 (at 2d) by the part enclosed between the radial lines from the column centre that are tangent to each opening; openings farther than 6d from the column are ignored, as in EC2 6.4.2(3).
  • Accuracy on experimental test series not used in training (nested cross-validation): see the manuscript and the GitHub repository.
  • Model inputs: d = h − cover, c, √f′c, ρ, a/d, Dop, Sop and the control perimeter ratio. A slab without openings is encoded as Dop = 0, Sop = 1000 mm, as in the training data.
  • Circular columns: use the side of the square column of equal perimeter, c = πD/4.
  • Dop is the opening dimension parallel to the column face (diameter for circular openings); Sop is the clear distance from the column face, or from the column corner for an opening on the diagonal, to the nearest point of the opening (0 for an opening adjacent to the column).
Accompanies the manuscript on the punching shear strength of reinforced concrete flat slabs with openings (H.Q. Nguyen, X.H. Nguyen, Q.S. Nguyen, D.D. Le, N.K. Le). Model, data and code: GitHub repository.
