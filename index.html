OpenPunch

Prediction of the punching shear capacity of reinforced concrete flat slabs with and without openings, using the optimized CatBoost model trained on 744 punching tests (185 slabs with openings). The calculation runs in your browser.

Research and preliminary-assessment tool. Predictions are data-driven, are reliable only within the range of the experimental database, and do not replace code-based design verification.
Model and inputs
  • Model: CatBoost regressor (1200 trees, depth 6), optimized by Bayesian search on 10-fold cross-validation MAE.
  • Held-out test performance (149 specimens): R² = 0.963, RMSE = 61.97 kN, MAE = 34.70 kN, MAPE = 12.83%; for the 44 test slabs with openings R² = 0.890, MAPE = 18.6%.
  • Inputs sent to the model: d = h − cover, c, √f′c, ρ, a/d, Dop and Sop. A slab without openings is encoded as Dop = 0, Sop = 1000 mm, as in the training data.
  • Circular columns: use the side of the square column of equal perimeter, c = πD/4.
  • Dop is the opening size; Sop is the clear distance from the column face to the nearest edge of the opening (0 for an opening adjacent to the column).
Accompanies the manuscript “Prediction of punching shear strength of reinforced concrete flat slabs with openings using ensemble machine learning” (H.Q. Nguyen, X.H. Nguyen, Q.S. Nguyen, D.D. Le, N.K. Le). Model, data and code: github.com/lekhuong/OpenPunch.
